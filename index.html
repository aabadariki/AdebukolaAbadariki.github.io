I am a cross-sector strategy and operations analyst with over six years of experience turning complex data into decisions that drive institutional and operational performance.

My career has spanned fintech, e-hailing, government, healthcare research, and public policy — not by accident, but because the problems that interest me most sit at the intersection of data, institutions, and systems. I have built reporting frameworks for government executives, led market expansions across South-West Nigeria, conducted cancer research analysis presented at international conferences, and mapped rare disease risk populations for pharmaceutical clients across UK cities.

As a Fellow of the Lateef Jakande Leadership Academy — selected from over 12,000 applicants — I have worked embedded inside Lagos State Government, supporting Executive Directors and Special Advisers on revenue systems, infrastructure execution, and operational performance. I currently serve as Vice President of the Class of Catalyst cohort.

My thinking is increasingly oriented toward how data, institutions, and productive sectors — infrastructure, agriculture, mining — can drive economic outcomes and shared prosperity. That is the problem I want to spend my career on.
